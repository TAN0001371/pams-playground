Friends in the Arcade

What a joy to find these friends playing together in a village, one Sunday afternoon. The arcade framed them perfectly — the deep shadow of the stone arch giving way to the sunlit courtyard beyond.

“Susan captured the easy companionship of the moment, the way their bodies leaned together in the game.”

From island shores to village lanes

A life spent noticing places

Childhood on a small Scottish island

Born to a marine biologist father, Susan grew up surrounded by water, weather and the changing light of the Hebrides.

New Zealand, England, USA, Australia

A life of movement and observation — living across continents, absorbing landscapes and cultures.

From programming to painting

Worked as a computer programmer, farmer and teacher before devoting herself fully to watercolour over twenty-five years ago.

A decade in south-west France

Lived for ten years in a French village, holding regular solo exhibitions since 2001.

Internationally collected

Work held in private collections across France, Italy, Germany, Holland, Belgium, Switzerland, the UK, USA, Canada, New Zealand and Australia.

People's Choice Award, 2022

Received at the Peninsula Studio Trail exhibition. Based now in the Melbourne area with her partner and two small dogs.

About the artist

A life spent noticing places

Susan Czermak is a watercolourist with a style that is colourful and bold. Her love of environments and their hues can be seen in her mood-filled paintings that capture everyday moments of interest.

A lover of travelling, Susan has lived in Scotland, New Zealand, England, USA, France and Australia. A realist painter inspired by the impressionists, her artworks capture her observations from around the globe. With a focus on land and streetscapes and interest in exploring light and shade, her paintings reflect on places and moments of delight.

She has exhibited extensively in France and Australia. Her art is internationally collected and can be found in private collections across Europe, the UK, USA, Canada, New Zealand and Australia.

Susan's studio is located in the Melbourne area of Victoria where she lives with her partner and two small dogs.
