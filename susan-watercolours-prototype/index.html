Watercolours painted from landscapes encountered across Australia, Europe and beyond

A life shaped by place

Susan Czermak has lived in Scotland, New Zealand, England, the USA, France and Australia. For over twenty-five years she has explored the world through watercolour — capturing the light, colour and feeling of places she has stood within. Her work is held in private collections across eleven countries. She accepts a limited number of commissions each year.
