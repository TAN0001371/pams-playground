The story behind

Friends in the Arcade

What a joy to find these friends playing together in a village, one Sunday afternoon. The arcade framed them perfectly — the deep shadow of the stone arch giving way to the sunlit courtyard beyond.

Illustrative story draft — to be confirmed with Susan

The painting captures the easy companionship of the moment: bodies leaning together in the game, the cool shadow of the arch a respite from the bright square beyond. Susan noticed how the light divided the scene — the players in half-darkness, the world beyond in full sun.

About the artist

The artist behind the paintings

Susan Czermak was born on a small island off the coast of Scotland. Her father worked as a marine biologist, and she spent an extraordinary childhood wandering the island and its seashore. For most of her life, however, she has lived in New Zealand and Australia.

She has worked variously as a computer programmer, a farmer and a teacher. From early childhood she had an interest in design and art, but these interests remained largely undeveloped until much later. She has been exploring the world and watercolour for the last twenty-five years, painting only subjects she has seen, trying to capture the mood and feelings evoked at the time.

Susan’s style is realist, and she hopes the work may sometimes appear somewhat impressionist. She is most interested in light and its effect on a subject — capturing the light and mood is more important to her than the details of the scene.

Since 2001 Susan has held regular solo exhibitions of her work, mainly in France, where she lived in a village in SW France for ten years. She won the People’s Choice Award at the 2022 PST Exhibition at the Mornington Studio.

Her art is held in private and official collections across Europe, the United Kingdom, USA, Canada, New Zealand and Australia. Susan’s studio is located in the Melbourne area of Victoria, where she lives with her partner and two small dogs.
