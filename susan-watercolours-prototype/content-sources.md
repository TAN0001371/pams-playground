## Biographical Information

All biographical content was sourced from Susan's public About page at `susanczermakwatercolours.com.au/about/`.

Key facts confirmed:
- Born on a small Scottish island
- Father was a marine biologist
- Lived in Scotland, New Zealand, England, USA, France and Australia
- Worked as a computer programmer, farmer and teacher
- Painting watercolours for approximately 25 years
- Lived 10 years in south-west France
- Regular solo exhibitions since 2001, mainly in France
- Work held in collections across multiple countries
- People's Choice Award, 2022 Peninsula Studio Trail
- Studio in Melbourne area, Victoria
